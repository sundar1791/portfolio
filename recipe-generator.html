Recipe Generator

An easy-to-use, Gen AI recipe generator

This was the first ever web app that I created, completely coded from scratch. I'm someone who loves to try new recipes, and more often than not, it's a pain to have to open recipe apps and browse through a catalogue of dishes that are either too complicated or not to my taste. I wanted to create a solution that's much simpler to use, reduces cognitive overload and yet gives me interesting dishes to try out.

I used the Gemini Pro API as a back-end to generate the recipe, while also using the help of Gemini to build the front end.

Here is the link to the web app -  https://recipe-app-440621.ew.r.appspot.com/
