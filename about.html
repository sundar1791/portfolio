About

Hola! I'm Sundar Subramanian

My career purpose is to design, build and scale innovative products and services that make people's lives better, while also making the planet more sustainable.
I'm an expert in product management, business model design and user experience design, and my work sits at the intersection of user needs, technical feasibility and business viability.
My biggest strength is an ability to zoom out and connect the dots between emerging trends across industries, markets, cultures and product categories to find unique strategic opportunities, and combine that with a hypothesis-driven approach to design and test new ideas and concepts, drive an agile-led product development, and execute an effective go-to-market plan.
In my 7-year journey so far in design and innovation, I have helped large companies across various sectors such as Consumer Electronics, Automotive, Technology, Agriculture, Banking, and Healthcare explore, design and launch innovative products that leverage technologies such as AI, IoT, Cloud and edge computing.
Being a constant learner, I am always on the lookout for new methodologies, frameworks, and perspectives. I occasionally share some of my thoughts on Design through my medium account - https://sundarsubramanian.medium.com/.
